I designed the CAP RATES NOW identity and adapted it for a truck wrap, rally signage, social posts, and video. The wrap was applied to an 18-wheeler parked outside the Local Government New Zealand conference.

The layouts used large type and contrasting colours so the message was readable at a distance and in event footage.
